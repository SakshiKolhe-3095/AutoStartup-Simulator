\# AutoStartup Simulator — Demo Script (Draft)



\## Goal

Show a judge/investor how a one-line idea becomes a full startup package in under 10 minutes.



\## Flow



1\. \*\*Intro (30s)\*\*

&#x20;  - "This is AutoStartup Simulator — type any startup idea, get market research,

&#x20;    financials, MVP + landing page, and a pitch deck, generated by a multi-agent AI swarm."



2\. \*\*Input (30s)\*\*

&#x20;  - Type sample idea into the form (use a pre-tested idea — TBD Wk10, backup ideas needed).

&#x20;  - Click Generate.



3\. \*\*Live pipeline (2-3 min)\*\*

&#x20;  - Show live-log streaming: CMO → CFO → CTO → CEO agents running in sequence.

&#x20;  - Narrate what's happening at each step.



4\. \*\*Results walkthrough (3-4 min)\*\*

&#x20;  - Show generated landing page (live link).

&#x20;  - Show investor Q\&A transcript — highlight a rebuttal exchange.

&#x20;  - Download and open pitch deck — walk through slides.



5\. \*\*Wrap-up (30s)\*\*

&#x20;  - Recap: idea → full startup package, zero manual work, fully free-tier stack.
